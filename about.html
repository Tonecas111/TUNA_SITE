JOÃO TUNA

Photographer and filmmaker. João Tuna studied photography, cinema and dramaturgy at Escola Artística António Arroio, Escola Superior de Teatro e Cinema and Faculdade de Letras da Universidade do Porto. He studied dramatic writing with António Mercado and Jean-Pierre Sarrazac. In 1990, he began his career in photography, focusing exclusively on portraiture and still photography for theatre and film. He has directed short films, institutional films, TV commercials, and film adaptations of performing arts productions. He has written screenplays, plays, and short texts on Art.
